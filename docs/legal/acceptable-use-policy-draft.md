# Acceptable Use Policy — proposed wording

**Status: DRAFT for UK legal review. Not legal advice and not yet in force.**
Prepared 12 November 2026. Once a solicitor has approved the wording, the platform
owner pastes it into *Platform administration → Applications → Policy*, sets a new
policy version and ticks "legal review completed". Until then intake stays closed.

---

## 1. Purpose

Vertex Seven ("we") provides AI sales and customer-service assistants to businesses.
This policy sets out which business activities we will and will not support. It applies
to every business that applies for, or uses, the service.

The policy is about **what a business does**. It is not about who owns or runs it. We
do not ask for, infer or take into account anyone's religion, belief, race, nationality,
sex, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other protected characteristic.

## 2. How we decide

1. Every new business is reviewed personally by an authorised Vertex Seven reviewer
   before it can buy a subscription. No payment is taken with an application.
2. We may use software, including AI, to gather publicly available information about an
   applicant's activities and present it to the reviewer. That software cannot approve or
   reject an application. A person always makes the decision.
3. We decide on the information you give us and on what your business publicly offers. If
   something is unclear we will ask you to clarify before deciding.

## 3. Activities we do not support

We do not provide the service to a business whose **primary activity** is any of the
following:

- (a) the sale, production or promotion of alcohol;
- (b) gambling, betting, lotteries or games of chance played for money;
- (c) pornography or sexually explicit entertainment or services;
- (d) interest-based lending as a main line of business, including payday lending;
- (e) tobacco, vaping products or recreational drugs;
- (f) weapons intended for use against people;
- (g) any activity that is unlawful in the United Kingdom or in the place it is carried on.

## 4. Businesses with mixed activities

Many businesses do several things. A business is not excluded merely because a small
part of what it does falls under section 3 (for example, a restaurant or supermarket
that also sells alcohol). We assess each such business individually, considering:

- how central the activity is to the business;
- whether the assistant would be asked to promote or sell it; and
- whether the assistant can be configured to leave that activity out.

Where we approve a mixed business we may make it a condition that the assistant does not
promote, sell or take orders for the excluded activity (see section 6).

## 5. Outcomes

After review we will tell you one of the following:

- **Approved.** You may choose a plan and pay.
- **More information needed.** We tell you what we need. You can reply from your
  application page; no new account or payment is required.
- **Not approved.** We tell you the outcome and the part of this policy it relates to.

You may correct your application or ask us to look again at any time from your
application page. A second review is carried out by a person.

If your business activities change materially after approval, tell us. We will review
the change before you make further purchases. A review does not by itself cancel a
subscription you already hold.

## 6. Eligibility is separate from what your assistant may do

Approval of a business does not mean every action is permitted. Separately from this
policy, your assistant will not, whatever its instructions:

- promote or sell anything listed in section 3;
- make statements it has no information to support, or impersonate a person;
- take payments, change orders or access customer accounts unless that specific
  capability has been enabled for your business;
- send marketing to people who have not agreed to receive it;
- process information in a way your agreement with us does not allow.

These limits are applied by the product itself and can be narrower for an individual
business where a condition under section 4 applies.

## 7. Suspension

If we find that a business is carrying on an activity in section 3 that was not
disclosed, or is using the assistant outside section 6, we may suspend the service
after giving notice and a reasonable opportunity to respond, except where the law or a
serious risk of harm requires us to act immediately.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version you accepted is recorded with your application.
We will tell existing customers about changes that affect them before they take effect.
