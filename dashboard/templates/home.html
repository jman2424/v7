FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What you need
to know.

How access, setup and integrations work.

What is V7 Agents?

V7 Agents is an AI sales assistant platform by Vertex Seven. It combines your business information with conversational assistance to help customers explore products, ask questions and decide what to do next.

What is Vertex Seven's official website?

Vertex Seven provides V7 Agents, a configurable AI sales assistant platform for business websites. Our official website is vertex-seven.com. V7 Agents is the product; Vertex Seven is the brand behind it.

Where does Vertex Seven provide its services?

Vertex Seven operates online and serves businesses across the United Kingdom. V7 Agents is managed through a business workspace and embedded on your website. We do not have a customer-facing premises or offer in-person customer appointments.

Which businesses can use V7 Agents?

Retailers, online stores, service businesses and businesses with several branches can configure their own agent. Add your catalog or services, FAQs, policies, opening hours and contact paths, then tailor the tone and next steps to your business. Booking, payments and other actions need the relevant integration; describing a service does not automatically connect it.

How can I tailor the assistant to my business?

Use your workspace to manage business knowledge, your agent playbook, response tone, customer goals and widget branding. Test typical customer questions before publishing and refine unclear answers. Your team remains responsible for accurate information and customer follow-up.

Do I need an account to explore this website?

No. This homepage and its illustrative examples are public. An account is required to manage a business workspace. When registration is available, verify your email to create a business or request to join one. Joining an existing company also requires its owner’s approval. If registration is unavailable, contact the platform operator for access.

Can I add it to my existing website?

Yes. Website chat can be embedded in your existing site. Your workspace includes an embed snippet and settings for your branding and approved website origins.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Every plan includes a WhatsApp agent. Its paid AI processing uses the same separately purchased prepaid GBP balance as website chat, rather than a fixed message allowance. WhatsApp phone numbers are not included: you need an eligible number and a configured Meta or Twilio provider connection. Number rental and WhatsApp provider charges are separate from the V7 subscription and are not covered by prepaid AI credit. Website chat works independently.

What happens when an allowance runs out?

Paid AI calls across website and WhatsApp pause when the available prepaid AI credit cannot safely cover the request or its cost is unknown. Saved business knowledge remains available, and the administrator sees a usage alert. There are no automatic extra charges.

What does my business need to provide?

Start with an accurate catalog, prices, FAQs, contact details, opening hours and delivery policies. Keep customer-facing information current and test your agent before sharing it with customers.

Will it replace my team?

It is designed to support your team with product guidance and common questions. Your people still handle complex requests, decisions and follow-up. AI responses can be incorrect, so review the experience and keep the underlying information accurate.

How do I get started?

If your company already has a workspace, sign in with your existing account or request to join with its owner’s approval. When registration is available, a new company can create a business after email verification. Choose a plan, complete onboarding and test your agent before launch. Contact the platform operator if you need access or setup help.
