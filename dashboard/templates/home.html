A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Get to know V7 Agents before you sign in.

What is V7 Agents?

V7 Agents is an AI sales assistant platform by Vertex Seven. It combines your business information with conversational assistance to help customers explore products, ask questions and decide what to do next.

Do I need an account to explore this website?

No. This homepage and its illustrative examples are public. An account is required to manage a business workspace. Your platform administrator provides that access; public account registration is not currently available.

Can I add it to my existing website?

Yes. Website chat can be embedded in your existing site. Your workspace includes an embed snippet and settings for your branding and approved website origins.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

WhatsApp integration is optional and requires your platform operator to configure a Meta or Twilio provider connection. Availability depends on your deployment and provider setup; website chat works independently.

What does my business need to provide?

Start with an accurate catalog, prices, FAQs, contact details, opening hours and delivery policies. Keep customer-facing information current and test your agent before sharing it with customers.

Will it replace my team?

It is designed to support your team with product guidance and common questions. Your people still handle complex requests, decisions and follow-up. AI responses can be incorrect, so review the experience and keep the underlying information accurate.

How do I get started?

If your company already has a workspace, sign in with the account supplied by your platform administrator. For a new company, the platform operator needs to arrange onboarding and configure your business before you can launch.
