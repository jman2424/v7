AI Budget: usage rates and payment terms

Your balance buys Vertex Seven AI processing services. It is separate from your subscription and setup fee, and is not an OpenAI account balance. No AI allowance is included.

Adding funds and controlling payments

Choose £10, £25 or £50 of V7 AI balance. Your balance increases by the full advertised amount after verified payment; payment fees are not deducted from it. Any applicable VAT and total payable are shown before payment. Existing unused balances carry forward.

Optional automatic recharge adds £5 when your settled balance falls below £2. The £5 payment includes any applicable VAT. It is off until you explicitly authorise a saved card and a £25, £50 or £100 monthly automatic purchase limit. It is available only after platform activation. Limits reset each calendar month in UTC and cover automatic purchases only; subscriptions and manual top-ups are separate. There is no fixed monthly AI payment.

Disable future recharges in AI Budget at any time. A payment already started may complete and will be recorded. A failed or uncertain payment pauses further automatic purchases for confirmation or review. Low-balance alerts are independent of automatic recharge.

How usage is charged

Requests use your selected model and may include business instructions, retrieved knowledge, conversation history and tool results. Longer inputs and replies cost more. A customer message can require several billable model calls. The rates below are deducted from your V7 balance, including any configured V7 service pricing. They are not direct-provider pass-through prices.

We reserve a safe amount before sending chargeable work, then settle using actual reported usage. Unknown outcomes remain held for review; a confirmed rejection before inference releases the reservation. If a verified final cost exceeds its reservation, service pauses for reconciliation. Further unpaid usage is prevented. We do not make automatic overage charges.

Other charges, refunds and help

Website, WhatsApp, email and phone AI work share your business's balance where enabled. Third-party phone numbers, telephony, Meta/provider messages and other external service fees are separate unless explicitly included. Browser speech playback does not create a V7 model-processing charge.

Refunds, disputes and payment reversals are reconciled against the original purchase. Already consumed services may require a balance review. Your statutory rights are unaffected. Contact support@vertex-seven.com about unused funds, billing questions or a disputed charge. See our terms and privacy notice.
