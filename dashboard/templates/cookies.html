Session storage and similar technologies

Cookie rules also cover browser storage. V7 uses the following session-storage entries for requested features; they are separate from optional website analytics. Session storage normally lasts for the current browser tab, although a browser's session-restore feature may preserve it. Where supported, browser messaging shares the recent conversation with other open tabs on the same business website. This does not send messages to the agent again, share unsent drafts, or use permanent local storage. Closing all chat tabs ends this cross-tab sharing.

Browser storage for a customer widget belongs to the V7 chat origin, even when the chat appears inside another business's website. That host business may use its own cookies and must explain those separately.

Optional public website analytics

When configured, Google Analytics loads only after your explicit analytics opt-in on the public homepage and solution pages. It does not run in the workspace, sign-in pages or customer chat. The application sends public page views with the page title and website address without query parameters or fragments; it does not send chat messages, form contents or account details. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled.

Google may also derive session and engagement information from permitted visits and receive technical information needed to handle the request. See Google’s privacy policy for how Google processes information.

Change your choice

lets you change the choices for this browser here, on the homepage or in the workspace. Uncheck analytics and save to stop subsequent measurement and remove its cookies while preserving your language and security choices. “Essential only” also removes the saved language preference. Closing the panel or pressing Escape does not grant optional consent. Browser controls can also remove cookies and session storage; blocking security cookies may prevent sign-in.

Withdrawal applies to future collection. It does not delete information already sent to Google. Choosing cookies in one browser does not change another browser's choices.

Trusted devices are a separate, explicit choice after successful sign-in. Use Account security to review or revoke them; removing a cookie from one browser alone does not revoke every device proof.

Other providers, voice and server records

A payment, social, messaging or sign-in provider may set cookies on its own service when you choose to visit it. Its own notice applies there. Browser speech features may use your browser or device's speech provider; microphone permission is separate from cookie consent.

V7 also stores operational conversation, account and security records on the server; those records are separate from browser cookie consent. Cookie rejection does not disable the security controls or records needed to provide a requested chat or account service.

Read the privacy information for business contacts, retention details and configured processors.
