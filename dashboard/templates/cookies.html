Cookie information

Cookies keep sign-in secure and remember the choices you request. Remembering your language and measuring public website visits are separate optional choices. No advertising cookies are used.

Optional public website analytics

When configured, Google Analytics loads only after your explicit analytics opt-in on the public homepage and solution pages. It does not run in the workspace, sign-in pages or customer chat. The application sends public page views with the page title and website address without query parameters or fragments; it does not send chat messages, form contents or account details. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled.

Google may also derive session and engagement information from permitted visits and receive technical information needed to handle the request. See Google’s privacy policy for how Google processes information.

Change your choice

Use Cookie preferences on the homepage or in the workspace to change your selection. Uncheck analytics and save to stop subsequent measurement and remove its cookies while preserving your language and security choices. “Essential only” also removes the saved language preference. Closing the panel or pressing Escape does not grant optional consent. Browser controls can also remove cookies; blocking security cookies may prevent sign-in.

Withdrawal applies to future collection. It does not delete information already sent to Google.

Trusted devices are a separate, explicit choice after successful sign-in. Use Account security to review or revoke them; removing a cookie from one browser alone does not revoke every device proof.

Other providers and server records

A payment or sign-in provider may set cookies on its own service when you choose to visit it. Its own notice applies there. V7 also stores operational conversation, account and security records on the server; those records are separate from browser cookie consent.

Read the privacy information for business contacts, retention details and configured processors.

Further information

Essential security functions, optional language storage and optional public website analytics are described separately so you can make an informed choice. This page describes the implementation and does not certify legal compliance.
