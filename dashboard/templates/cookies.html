YOUR BROWSER CHOICES

Cookie information

Cookies keep sign-in secure and remember the choices you request. The application does not add advertising or visitor-tracking cookies.

Cookies used by V7

CookiePurposeDuration and choice
{{ session_cookie }}Signed session data for sign-in, security challenges and CSRF protection.Essential security. Anonymous sessions may end with the browser. After sign-in, the configured browser duration is up to {{ '%g'|format(cookie_hours) }} hours; effective management access is limited to {{ '%g'|format(account_hours) }} hours, or can end sooner on logout or account changes.

v7_languageRemembers your selected language.Optional. Set for up to 180 days only after “Save my preferences”. Choosing “Essential only” removes it. Without a saved preference, language changes apply to the current page experience.

Change your choice

Use Cookie preferences on the homepage or in the workspace to change your selection. Essential-only leaves sign-in/security functions available and removes saved optional language preferences. Browser controls can also remove cookies; blocking security cookies may prevent sign-in.

Trusted devices are a separate, explicit choice after successful sign-in. Use Account security to review or revoke them; removing a cookie from one browser alone does not revoke every device proof.

Other providers and server records

A payment or sign-in provider may set cookies on its own service when you choose to visit it. Its own notice applies there. V7 also stores operational conversation, account and security records on the server; those records are separate from browser cookie consent.

Read the privacy information for business contacts, retention details and configured processors.
