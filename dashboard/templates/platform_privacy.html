When a business uses V7 to serve its own customers, that business normally decides why its customer information is collected and is the controller for those conversations. For a conversation with another business's agent, contact that business first; we can help route a request to it. Its privacy notice applies alongside this explanation of the platform.

What information we use

  • Accounts and security: names, email addresses, account identifiers, roles, business details, sign-in and MFA records, linked-account identifiers and security events. Passwords are stored as hashes, not readable passwords.
  • Enquiries and conversations: messages, assistant replies, information you choose to share, contact and callback details, booking or quote requests, selected offerings and recorded clicks on suggested links. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not needed for your enquiry.
  • Business configuration: the products, services, policies, website content, contact paths and other information an authorised business user supplies or imports.
  • Payments and usage: plan and subscription information, billing contact information, payment and invoice references, transaction status, credit balances, and model/token usage and costs. Stripe handles payment details on its payment pages; V7 does not need your full card number.
  • Technical records: IP addresses, request identifiers, timestamps, browser information, errors and access/audit records used to run and protect the service.
  • Optional analytics: public marketing-page views and related device/session information when you opt in. Cookie choices and browser storage are explained in the cookie policy.

We receive information from you, authorised users of your business account, customer conversations, public business webpages you ask us to import, and payment, messaging or sign-in providers used for your requested service.

We consider the impact on individuals when relying on those interests. You may object to this processing. Creating an account or contacting us is not consent to unrelated marketing. We do not sell personal information or use advertising cookies on this website.

AI, memory and voice

When enabled and funded, AI providers receive the message and the relevant conversation context and business information needed to answer it. A business can configure conversation-memory limits; a context window is not the same as deletion of the stored conversation or activity records.

AI replies can be wrong. V7 is intended to assist with business enquiries, and you can ask for human assistance. We do not use the assistant to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. A suggestion or navigation button is not confirmation that a booking, purchase, refund or account change succeeded.

Browser dictation and read-aloud features may use your browser or device's speech service and its provider. If server transcription is used, V7 handles the audio in memory and sends it to the enabled transcription provider; the resulting text can be stored as part of the conversation. V7 does not create an audio archive in that transcription flow. A WhatsApp or other messaging provider may retain the original message under its own policy. Microphone access requires your browser permission.

Your rights and how to make a request

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction or a portable copy of your personal information. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Email the privacy contact above. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity and locate the correct records. We normally respond within one month; if an extension or exception applies, we explain it. Signed-in users can download their account identity and preference metadata; this limited download is not a complete export of customer or business records.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. You do not have to contact us first, although we welcome the chance to address your concern.

Security and appropriate use

V7 uses access controls, tenant separation, MFA, protected account sessions and audit records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in details private and report suspected account access to support. The platform is intended for business users and their customer enquiries; do not use it to submit children's information or sensitive records unnecessarily.
