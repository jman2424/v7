Information used by the service

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the processing, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information. Contact the controller above to make a request or withdraw consent where consent is used.

You may have a right to object to processing. Raise an objection with the controller so it can review the relevant purpose and lawful basis.

Signed-in users can download their own account identity and preference metadata. This limited export does not include customer conversations, other accounts or business records; requests for those require a controller review.

For UK data protection concerns, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office. Other locations may have a local regulator.
